Dr Claudio Lombardi

Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Law, University of Aberdeen

I work on competition law, the regulation of digital platforms and empirical legal studies, combining doctrinal analysis with quantitative methods to test whether legal promises — of redress, of contestable markets — hold up in practice.

I am Associate Director of the Centre for Commercial Law and Impact Lead for the School of Law. I have advised governments and international organisations on competition policy, and work in research partnership with UNCTAD, the Competition and Markets Authority and the Law Society of Scotland.
